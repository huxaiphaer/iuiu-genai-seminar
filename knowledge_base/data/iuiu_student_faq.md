# IUIU Student FAQ (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

> This is made-up sample data for the seminar. It is NOT official university
> information. Do not use real, personal, or examination data in the workshop.

## Library

The main library is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and on
Saturday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
Students may borrow up to five books for two weeks.

## Registration

Course registration opens two weeks before each semester and closes on the
Friday of the first week of lectures. Late registration attracts a fee of
50,000 UGX. Registration is done through the student portal.

## Grading

Marks are reported as: A (80-100), B+ (75-79), B (70-74), C+ (65-69),
C (60-64), D (50-59) and F (below 50). A grade of D or above is a pass.

## Fees

Tuition must be at least 60% paid before a student can sit end-of-semester
examinations. Payment is made through the approved bank and uploaded to the
portal.

## Contacts

For academic issues, email the Faculty of Science office. For portal or
password problems, contact the ICT help desk during working hours.
